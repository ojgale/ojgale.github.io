## Context

San Diego and Tijuana shared the World Design Capital title in 2024: one region, two countries, two languages and civic systems that don't always line up. The question for the Design Lab was what would still be standing after the year of events ended.

## Insight

What looks like participation on paper has to work across borders, languages and institutions that rarely share a calendar. Partners needed structure they could lean on without it flattening their local context. [Add one specific moment from a DAN meeting or site visit that showed you this.]

## Intervention

I managed the Legacy Program and co-led its six Design to Action Networks: Arts & Culture, Planning & Placemaking, Climate & Sustainability, Science & Technology, Health & Wellbeing, and Youth & Education. Each network guided community-based impact projects, measured progress, shared it publicly and gathered feedback from residents and experts.

To run it with a lean staff, I built the operating systems underneath: project intake, impact reporting, facilitator guides and action plan templates, plus 30+ site visits, field trips and network meetings.

## Outcome

- 63 binational impact projects supported
- 300+ civic and design leaders engaged
- 3,600+ hours of design-driven support for public initiatives
- 200+ new connections for grants, venues, speakers and partnerships; 8+ new grants
- The Design to Action Networks received the 2024 Don Norman Design Award

> "Building structure around improvisation: toolkits and learning sessions that supported partners without flattening local context."
