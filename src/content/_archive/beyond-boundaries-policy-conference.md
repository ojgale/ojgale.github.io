## Context

World Design Capital 2024 needed a policy moment: a place where designers, officials and researchers from both sides of the border could talk about how design shows up in public decisions. The Design Lab took it on with a small team, few resources and little conference experience.

## Intervention

I co-led the conference across 18 months of development. I shaped the thematic framing and session structure, led content strategy, and co-developed the brand and voice with our design team. I managed speakers, marketing, sales, venue experience and technical production.

Sessions covered universal basic healthcare, resilient communities, health innovation, policy in North America and the creative economy. I taught myself WordPress, Elementor and Whova to launch the conference's public presence without leaning on overextended teammates.
